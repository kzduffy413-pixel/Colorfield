Residence 301 at The Colorfield is a 3,575 square foot corner residence on the third floor, designed by Alterstudio Architecture with an elevated eastern exposure and views toward Downtown Austin and the Texas Capitol. Floor to ceiling curtain wall glass brings abundant natural light into the residence while connecting the interiors to the surrounding cityscape.

The single level floor plan offers three bedrooms, three and one half baths, and a versatile den ideal for a private office, media room, library, or additional guest space. Two secondary bedrooms each open to a private balcony, while a separate covered terrace extends the main living space outdoors.

The primary suite is designed as a private retreat with a generous walk-in closet and spa inspired bath featuring a freestanding soaking tub and dual vanities. Residence 301 features The Colorfield's darker interior palette, pairing wide plank white oak flooring with walnut inspired and black millwork, darker tile and stone selections, and light countertops for a sophisticated sense of contrast.

The chef's kitchen is appointed with a complete Gaggenau appliance suite, including built-in refrigerator and freezer, gas cooktop, wall oven, coffee system, dishwasher, and dual wine columns. A generous laundry room provides built-in cabinetry, storage, and extensive countertop space. Two reserved garage parking spaces are included.

The Colorfield is a boutique collection of just ten luxury condominiums in Clarksville, in the heart of Old West Austin. Residents enjoy a secure parking garage, staffed concierge and front desk, and two elevators serving the building, all within minutes of Downtown Austin, the Texas Capitol, Lady Bird Lake, and some of Austin's most celebrated restaurants, shops, parks, and cultural destinations.

Construction is complete, and Residence 301 is available now.
