Designed for light, not density.

Every detail at The Colorfield reflects a commitment to thoughtful architecture and enduring design, brought to life by Alterstudio Architecture, one of Austin's most decorated design firms with more than 150 design awards to its name. Expansive walls of glass welcome natural light while framing sweeping eastern views of downtown Austin and the Texas Capitol.

Generous living spaces flow effortlessly to covered outdoor terraces, creating homes designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. Rather than maximizing the number of residences, The Colorfield prioritizes space, privacy, and livability, an experience rarely found in condominium living.

Welcome to the Neighborhood

Clarksville, at the pulse of downtown.

Located in Clarksville, one of Austin's most desired neighborhoods, The Colorfield connects residents to the pulse of downtown living. The site carries real history: home to HOPE Outdoor Gallery from 2010 until its close in January 2019, the beloved community art park that made this stretch of Baylor Street a destination for muralists and locals alike. That legacy lives on inside the building itself, where a commissioned mural by internationally recognized artist Ysabel Lemay graces the entrance floor, honoring the site's creative past. Enjoy easy access to premium shopping, dining, and entertainment, all from a residential address that rewards those who value a private, exclusive lifestyle.
