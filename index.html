The Design

Architecture with intention.

Designed by Alterstudio Architecture, The Colorfield brings a distinctly architectural approach to residential living in the heart of Clarksville. Expansive walls of glass draw natural light deep into each residence while framing views toward downtown Austin and the Texas Capitol.

Generous interiors open to private outdoor living spaces, creating a natural connection between inside and out. Wide plank white oak floors bring warmth throughout each residence, balancing the building's clean architectural lines with a distinctly residential character.

History

A legacy preserved.

From 2010 until its closure in 2019, this site was home to HOPE Outdoor Gallery, the community art park that made this stretch of Baylor Street a destination for artists and visitors alike.

That legacy continues inside The Colorfield, where a commissioned work by internationally recognized artist Ysabel LeMay carries the site's creative spirit into its next chapter.

The Neighborhood

Clarksville, in the heart of Old West Austin.

Located in Clarksville within Old West Austin, The Colorfield places downtown, Lady Bird Lake, the Texas Capitol, and some of Austin's most beloved restaurants and neighborhood destinations within easy reach.
